### Findings (for the author, not changed here: test-only PR)
- **Q1 (low, defense in depth):** `restoreVolume` calls itself the second barrier ("notes alone prove nothing"), but it does not compare:
  - the marker's **install id** (`parts[1]`) with `Config.InstallID`;
  - the **trigger** (`:m`) with the job row;
  - the **size and time** with the job row.

  A volume that the adapter classifies `plumb` with a foreign install marker, another size or another trigger is planned (probe: 201 for each). The real adapter (`cmd/plumb/restoreguest.go`) uses `backup.Classify` with the recorded rows, which checks all four, so the gap opens only with a broken adapter. **Fix:** compare `parts[1]` with `e.cfg.InstallID` and the trigger with `run.Trigger`. For size and time, expose `size`/`ctime` on `store.GuestBackupRun` and compare. Or say in the comment that the adapter is the only check for these.
- The `Revoke` failure itself does not end with the "way back" text. That is right: the credential may still be valid. Its text says "start the guest in Proxmox, or plan the restore again".
