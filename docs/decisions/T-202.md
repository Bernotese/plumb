- D-366 · 2026-11-14 · amended (pl-backend, T-180, T-202; review of #287). The per-agent limit of drift reports is taken right after the strict decode of the report (body cap `wire.MaxReportBytes`, 1 MiB), before validation, store reads and the report handler; only `type: drift` counts, results, progress, confirms, hellos and address reports never do. After a 429 (or 503) on a drift send the agent arms one timer at `Retry-After` (at least 1 s, at most 2 min, plus up to 10 % jitter) in its drift loop and then sends the kept items once; a new 429 arms it again; the timer never runs beside a check and ends with the drift loop · the type is needed to keep results and confirms free, and a peek at `type` scans the whole body like the decode does and could read another type than the decode; "sends it after the wait" needs a timer, because a refused drift was otherwise sent only at the next check (up to 15 min) or at a reconnect
