- D-320 · 2026-11-13 · proposed (pl-backend, T-113). After a restore the guest poll opens `guest:missing` (change `guest_id` null → VMID, `destructive`) when a bound guest is `ErrNotFound`, also in restored mode, and revokes the agent of that Target once per process (a repeat costs nothing); it resolves as gone when the guest is back. A bound VMID whose guest has another name holds another guest (Proxmox gave the VMID of a guest that the old controller deleted to a new one): the poll treats it as `guest:missing` (also revoke) and lists that guest in `unknown_guests`; and every guest step of a Run after a restore first checks that the VMID still holds a guest with the Target's name, else the step fails and nothing is written, also for a delete (review H2 of #222). `guest:missing` stays for the life of a restored controller, also long after the confirm, on purpose: a gone guest of a restored controller always deserves an Issue (review L1 of #222). `agent:silent` (change `hello` null → "any", `read`) opens at a poll from 10 minutes after the confirm for a running guest whose agent is offline and said no hello since the restore (an agent that connected in restored mode stays connected and says no new hello, review M1 of #222); a hello or a stopped guest resolves it; the poll reads the restore record once (review L3). Both are opened by `system` and offer only the ignore actions (`issue.IgnoreOnly`, like `guest:address`). `GET /restore` lists `unknown_guests`: pool guests (new read-only `Driver.ListGuests`) that are no Target or Image binding, no build guest and no template of an Image of the head; read at most once a minute; Plumb never touches them · the hello time is stored, so the check survives a restart of serve after the confirm
