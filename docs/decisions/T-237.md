- D-436 · 2026-11-16 · proposed (pl-architect, T-237; refines D-423; pl-reviewer checks). A data disk in `guest.disks` is `{slot, size_gb, path?}`: the **slot** (1 … 3) is its identity (VM `scsiN` with serial `plumb-N`, LXC `mpN`), never the position in the list. A missing `disks` field means Plumb does not manage data disks; `[]` or a list means Plumb manages slots 1 … 3, and a disk in a slot that the list does not name is drift. Removing the field deletes nothing. `path` is create-only per slot · with the position as identity, removing disk 1 of 2 would resize disk 2 into slot 1 and delete the wrong disk; "no field = unmanaged" follows `tags`, `power` and `backup` (M2 of #325); a create-only path keeps T-238 small and never moves a mount under running data
