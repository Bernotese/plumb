- D-419 · 2026-11-15 · accepted (pl-lead, T-220; D-400, D-403, D-370). The address of a more NIC N of a VM is a `network` resource `nicN` that only the render makes from `guest.nics` (the engine's render, with Plumb's MAC of the card from the install id): attributes `mac` (only Plumb's form `02:…`), `dhcp` (written as `dhcp4` always) and `gateway` (only with a static address, refused while the main NIC has the default route). A Block or an override with `mac` is refused by the server. The agent finds the card by that MAC (the permanent MAC when the kernel reports one), never the main NIC, and writes under the single netplan entry that names the card, or an own entry `plumb-<name>` that matches the MAC; an entry that names the card only by a glob, two entries, or no card refuses with nothing changed. Every card of `guest.nics` gets its resource, also without an address · no MAC of a person or a guest ever reaches a netplan match; a card that comes back with the same MAC never keeps an old file; a glob entry would let two cards share their values
