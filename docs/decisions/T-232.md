A restore Run first takes a safety backup of the guest as it is (trigger `pre-restore`, marker `:r`, not counted by retention, kept until the next restore that works or a person's delete); if it fails, nothing is stopped, revoked or restored. A restore that fails after Proxmox began says that the guest may have no disk, names the safety backup (or another backup) as the way back, and opens the ignore-only Issue `guest:restore-failed`, which a restore that works resolves. The Plan says that Proxmox removes the guest's disks before it writes the backup. The safety backup may be skipped only for a broken guest (no disk, or that Issue open), human-only like the restore.
