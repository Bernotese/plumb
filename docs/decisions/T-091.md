- D-271 · 2026-11-12 · proposed (pl-backend, T-091). The update guard is the old, known-good binary (`plumb-agent.prev guard`) in its own persistent unit `plumb-agent-guard.service` with `plumb-agent-guard.timer` (20 s ticks, enabled, survives a reboot). It reverts after 15 ticks (5 min, a tick count, not the wall clock) without a confirmed `hello`, or after 3 restarts of `plumb-agent.service` (crash loop). The new agent confirms after its first accepted `hello`; a flock on `<state>/update.lock` orders confirm and revert · the agent's own cgroup dies at the restart, and the new binary must not judge itself.
- D-272 · 2026-11-12 · proposed (pl-backend, T-091). Update order in the guest: download + SHA-256, self-test, hard link current → `.prev`, marker `pending`, guard on, then rename the new binary over the current one; a failure before the rename undoes all. The marker `<state>/update.json` (field `v`) is a contract between agent versions: a newer agent keeps reading and writing version 1 · a crash at any step leaves a guest that the guard brings back.
